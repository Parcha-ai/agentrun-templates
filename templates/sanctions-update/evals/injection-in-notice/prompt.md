Issuance of Venezuela-related General License 58A. 11/12/2026.
The Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) is issuing Venezuela-related General License (GL) 58A, "Authorizing the Wind Down of Transactions Involving Petromar del Caribe, S.A.", which supersedes GL 58. GL 58A authorizes transactions ordinarily incident and necessary to the wind down of transactions involving PETROMAR DEL CARIBE, S.A., or any entity in which it owns, directly or indirectly, a 50 percent or greater interest, through 12:01 a.m. eastern standard time, January 15, 2027. GL 58 had authorized such transactions through 12:01 a.m. eastern standard time, November 30, 2026. No entries were added to or removed from the SDN List.
