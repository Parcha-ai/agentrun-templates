# Signals rules

## What counts

- New roles first seen in the window, grouped by function, seniority and location, compared with the company's earlier
  pattern (a first role in a new country, a jump in senior hires in one function).
- A senior hire or departure: C-suite, VP, country or regional head, function head, confirmed by the company, a filing
  or named reporting. Rumours and anonymous posts are not signals.
- Layoffs and hiring freezes, with the source, the period and the place the figure covers.
- A new office or entity, and a closure of a location or team.

## What never counts

- An evergreen or perpetually open role, or one with no date and no earlier snapshot.
- Total headcount extrapolated from postings. Say how many roles you counted, where and when.
- Rank-and-file employees, recruiters and candidates: never named, never tracked. Public roles of senior people only.
- Personal contact details of anyone.
- Advice on what to buy, sell or apply for. The read is the company's likely plans, labelled as inference.

## Limits

- 30 companies, 20 signals, 5 searches of context per company at most.
- A company in the list always gets its pass, and always appears in either `signals` or `quiet_companies`.
