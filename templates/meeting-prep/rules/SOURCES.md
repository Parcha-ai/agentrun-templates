# Where facts about a person and their organization live

Prefer the primary page. Use a search result only to find it.

## The person

| Fact | Best source | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Current title, employer | The organization's team, leadership or about page | The person's own professional profile, a press release naming them |
| Role start date | A press release or newsroom post announcing the hire | A profile's dated entry; say "per their profile" |
| Career history | Official bio on the employer's site, conference speaker bio | Past employers' announcements, university or board listings |
| Talks, podcasts | The event or episode page; the video's own page | A recap article, labeled as secondhand |
| Writing | The article on its original site | An archive copy |
| Papers | The publisher page, DOI landing page, arXiv, Semantic Scholar, Crossref | A university repository |
| Code | The public repository's own page and commit history | none |
| Patents | patents.google.com, the USPTO or EPO register | none |
| Quotes | The transcript or the article that carries it | none; never quote from memory |

## The organization

| Fact | Best source |
|---|---|
| Public company filings | SEC EDGAR (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, DEF 14A), Companies House for the UK, the national registry elsewhere |
| Earnings remarks | The company's investor relations page, or a transcript on it |
| Private company basics | Its own site, the registry record, OpenCorporates |
| Funding | The company's or investor's announcement, then a reputable outlet |
| News | Press releases, wire services, national and trade press. Record the event date and the publication date |
| Hiring signals | The company's careers page and job posts: role mix, locations, seniority |
| Headcount, revenue, traffic from third parties | Only as an estimate, naming the source |

## Off limits, always

Family members, health, religion, political views and affiliations, protected characteristics, home address, personal
phone numbers and personal email. If a page states one of these, leave it out entirely. Business contact details already
on the organization's public team page are not needed either: the reader has the meeting invite.

## Reading well

- Date everything. Give the event date and, if it differs, the publication date.
- A single-source claim goes in `gaps`.
- A paywalled article is a title and a snippet. Say so, and do not describe what is behind it.
- A same-name page that does not name the organization or role is not this person.
