WASHINGTON, November 12, 2026. The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) assessed a $750,000 civil money penalty against Lumenpay Transfers LLC, a money services business in Miami, Florida, for failing to register with FinCEN as a money services business and for failing to file Currency Transaction Reports on 1,260 cash transactions over $10,000 between March 2022 and August 2024. FinCEN made findings in the assessment; Lumenpay consented to the assessment and admitted the facts set out in it. The violations came to light during an examination by the IRS Small Business/Self-Employed Division. Lumenpay must hire an independent auditor to test its CTR filing process for two years. Assessment dated November 10, 2026.
