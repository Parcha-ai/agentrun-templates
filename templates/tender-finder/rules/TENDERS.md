# Tenders rules

## Portals to start from, by region

Start from the official portal. Many publish plain HTML or an open API; read what you can and mark the rest.

- EU and EEA: TED (ted.europa.eu) for notices above the EU thresholds, then each country's national portal.
- UK: Find a Tender (find-tender.service.gov.uk) and Contracts Finder (contractsfinder.service.gov.uk).
- US: SAM.gov for federal notices (it often needs an account for detail: then `needs_login`), then the state's portal.
- Canada: CanadaBuys. Australia: AusTender. Other countries: one search for the official portal.
- Development funding: UNGM, World Bank and regional development banks, only for development-funded work or when asked.

## Status and deadlines

- Open means the notice page says so today. Closed, cancelled, suspended and awarded are not listed.
- The deadline is as the notice states it, with the time and zone. No zone: write `zone not stated`. Never convert a
  deadline into another zone.
- An amendment or corrigendum that moves the deadline wins over the first notice.

## What never goes in

- A title, buyer, value, deadline or winner the notice page did not show. Missing is `not stated`.
- A tender you did not open on its official page this run.
- Personal details beyond the professional contact a notice publishes for bidders.
- Legal or procurement advice. Questions of law go to the bid or legal team.
- Any action on a portal: no registration, login, download of gated documents, message or bid.

## Limits

30 new tenders, 60 still-open lines, 15 near misses, 5 next actions.
