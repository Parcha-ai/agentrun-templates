# Earnings rules

## Numbers

- Every number has its source and period: "Q3 FY2026, quarter ended Sep 30". Say reported or constant currency, and give
  growth as the company presents it.
- A non-GAAP or adjusted figure sits next to its GAAP or IFRS equivalent, under the company's own label.
- Guidance: the old range, the new range and the metric. Maintained means the company said so. Silence is not_given.

## Consensus

- Named source and the date compiled, or the words "consensus not verified". Never an unnamed consensus.
- Estimates, previews and leaks for a quarter not yet reported are never a report. Label them and leave them out.

## Quotes

- Word for word, with speaker and title, from a transcript or replay you read. Never paraphrase inside quotation marks.
- No transcript readable: summarize the release and label the summary. No quote.

## Stock reaction

- A percentage with its date and session ("fell 6.2% on Oct 29, first full session after results"). After-hours or
  intraday only when labelled as such.

## What never goes in

- Buy, sell or hold advice, price targets, valuation calls.
- A number, quote, date or range a source you read did not show.
- Private individuals beyond executives and analysts speaking on the record in their professional role.
- Two companies merged: each fact comes from the one issuer you resolved.

## Limits

30 companies, 4 quotes per report, 5 Q&A themes.
