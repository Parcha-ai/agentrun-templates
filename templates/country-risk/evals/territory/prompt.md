<h1>Ukraine-/Russia-Related Sanctions</h1>

<h1>Crimea and Sevastopol: EU restrictive measures</h1>
<p>In response to the illegal annexation of Crimea and Sevastopol, the EU applies restrictive measures under Council
Decision 2014/386/CFSP and Council Regulation (EU) No 692/2014, including an import ban on goods originating in Crimea or
Sevastopol, restrictions on trade and investment in certain sectors, and a ban on supplying tourism services. The
measures are renewed annually; the current renewal runs to 2027-06-23.</p>
