# What makes an item

The field-weekly skill reads this before every edition. Edit it like code. What the owner tells the agent later is kept
in its learning and wins over this file where they differ.

## Items

- **paper / preprint**: a new result, method, benchmark, replication or failed replication, major review. A preprint is
  `peer_reviewed: false` and the report says "Preprint (not peer-reviewed)". A preprint since published says where.
- **dataset / tool**: a public dataset, benchmark, software release or model researchers can use, from its record
  (Zenodo, Figshare, Dryad, Hugging Face, GitHub release).
- **funding_call**: an open call from the funder's own page: funder, programme, reference, deadline with time and time
  zone, eligibility in brief, award size when stated.
- **conference_deadline**: from the conference's own CFP page: what is due, the date and time zone ("AoE" is Anywhere on
  Earth), and an extension when the page shows one.
- **policy**: a funder, journal or society rule that shapes the field (data sharing, open access).
- **retraction**: from the journal's notice or Retraction Watch.

Citation counts, social buzz and press coverage are never items; they may be a detail inside one.

## Where to look

Preprint servers (arXiv listings for the field's categories, bioRxiv, medRxiv, SSRN, NBER, PsyArXiv, ChemRxiv), journal
online-first pages, Crossref, OpenAlex or PubMed to confirm dates and DOIs, data repositories, funders' call pages (NIH
Guide, NSF, UKRI, ERC, Wellcome), the conference's own CFP page (trackers are pointers only), Retraction Watch.

## Reporting a paper

- One sentence on what it adds, one on how: data, sample size, model or design.
- The key number with its unit, baseline and dataset, as the authors state it, prefixed "the authors report". Never round
  a claim into a stronger one.
- `read`: `abstract` when that is all you could open, and say so in `caveats`.
- Flag small samples, missing controls, preclinical or animal results, and press releases that overstate the paper. The
  press release's wording never becomes the item's claim.
- Authors appear as they are on the publication. Nothing personal about them.

## Dates

Posting date and journal date are different; a call's announcement date and its deadline are different. A deadline that
has passed is not an open call. An item dated before the window is not this week's, unless it is a call or deadline still
open and announced in the last 30 days, dated as such.
