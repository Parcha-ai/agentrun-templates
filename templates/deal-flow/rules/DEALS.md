# Deals rules

## What counts

Announced, signed, closed, filed, priced or listed inside the window, in the sector: priced rounds, new funds that
invest in it, acquisitions, mergers, divestitures, buyouts, IPO filings and listings, secondary sales, shutdowns.
A regulator's clearance, block or condition on a pending deal is an event. Share-price moves and analyst targets are
not deals.

## How to state one

- Status is exact: a filing is not a listing, a signed deal is not a closed one. Use the word the primary source uses.
- Every number has its currency and period ("FY2025 revenue", "ARR as of Q2"). Convert currency only with the rate and
  the date stated.
- A figure only a data provider or an outlet gives carries that name in `amount_basis`.
- Rationale is attributed to whoever stated it. Vendor claims are labelled as claims.

## What never goes in

- An estimated or derived figure, such as a valuation worked out from a round size.
- A rumour stated as done. Rumours are `rumored` with the outlet named.
- A deal you cannot date to the window.
- Personal details of private individuals or employees hit by a shutdown. Founders, executives and investors appear
  in their professional roles only.
- Advice to buy, sell or price a security.

## Limits

20 deals, 6 searches, 10 fetches.
