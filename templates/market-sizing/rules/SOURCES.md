# Where market numbers live

Use a search result only to find the primary page.

## Published estimates

Analyst firms publish a headline figure in a press release or a report summary page and sell the rest: Gartner, IDC,
Grand View Research, MarketsandMarkets, Mordor Intelligence, Fortune Business Insights, Statista previews. What you read
free is `summary_only` or `paywalled`. Trade associations, industry bodies, central banks and government studies often
publish the full figure free.

Record for each: the value as stated, the data year, the CAGR and forecast year, the scope, the publisher, the
publication date. The data year is usually in the sentence ("valued at USD X billion in 2024"), not the date at the top.

## Official data

| Need | Source |
|---|---|
| US buyers and establishments | US Census Bureau: Economic Census, County Business Patterns, Annual Business Survey |
| US employment, wages, prices | Bureau of Labor Statistics |
| US output by industry | Bureau of Economic Analysis |
| EU structure of an industry | Eurostat: Structural Business Statistics, PRODCOM |
| UK | Office for National Statistics |
| Cross-country | OECD, World Bank, IMF |
| Traded goods | UN Comtrade (by HS code) |
| Regulated markets | The regulator or licensing registry: FDA, FCC, energy regulators, financial regulators |
| Industry codes | NAICS (census.gov/naics), NACE (Eurostat), HS (WCO), SIC |

## Companies

| Need | Source |
|---|---|
| Segment revenue, customers, revenue per customer | SEC EDGAR 10-K and 10-Q, annual reports, investor decks, earnings transcripts |
| Players in a category | Review-site category grids (G2, Capterra, TrustRadius), "alternatives to" pages, trade press |
| Pricing model | The company's own pricing page; "contact sales" when none |
| Funding | The company's or investor's announcement, then a reputable outlet; a database only as a cross-check |
| Size proxies | Stated headcount, LinkedIn ranges, job-post volume, filings. Mark est. with the basis |

## Reading well

- A 2026 report quoting a 2023 value is a 2023 figure.
- A paywalled figure seen in a summary is `summary_only`. Do not describe the report's contents beyond it.
- A convert-and-adjust step needs a stated rate and date, written down.
