## Bottom line
The US market for paid appointment-reminder software at independent veterinary clinics was about $30M in 2025 (range
$14M to $50M): 40,000 clinics, 25% paying, $3,000 a year [S1][S2][S3]. TAM is about $120M. A new entrant's SOM is about
$3M, which is inference. Confidence is medium: the price is one vendor's.

## Bottom-up build
Formula: clinics x adoption x spend.

| Input | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Clinics | 40,000 | fact [S1] |
| Adoption | 25% (15% to 35%) | fact [S2] |
| Spend per clinic | $3,000 (2,400 to 3,600) | inference [S3] |

40,000 x 0.25 = 10,000 clinics; 10,000 x $3,000 = $30M.

## TAM / SAM / SOM
