# Where facts about a company live

Use a search result only to find the primary page. Prefer the primary page.

| Fact | Best source | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Legal entity, HQ | The site's footer, terms or imprint; the national registry | OpenCorporates, GLEIF |
| UK registry | find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk (Companies House) | none |
| US public filings | sec.gov/edgar (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, S-1, DEF 14A) | The company's investor relations page |
| US private entities | The state registry, via OpenCorporates | The company's own terms |
| Products, pricing | The company's own product and pricing pages | Cloud marketplace listings (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud); label as indirect |
| Customers | Logos and case studies on its own site; its press releases | A customer's own announcement |
| Funding | The company's or investor's announcement | A reputable outlet, then a database (Crunchbase, PitchBook, Dealroom) as a cross-check only |
| Revenue, headcount | Filings or the company's own statement | Third-party estimates, labeled with the source |
| News | Press releases, wire services, national and trade press | none |
| Leaders' priorities | Earnings-call transcripts, shareholder letters, conference pages, podcast episode pages | The article quoting them, labeled secondhand |
| Hiring | The careers page and job posts | Job boards |
| Tech stack | Job descriptions, the engineering blog, the integrations page | none |
| Product reviews | G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, app stores | Reddit, Hacker News for patterns, never one post |
| Employer reviews | Glassdoor and similar, with sample size and date | none |
| Litigation, regulation | The relevant regulator's or court's public page | A news report that names the case |

## Reading well

- Give the event date and, if different, the publication date. A 2025 article quoting a 2023 figure is a 2023 figure.
- Mark estimates `estimate` and name whose. Mark single-source numbers in `gaps`.
- A paywalled page is a title and a snippet. Say so, and do not describe what is behind it.
- People: name, role, tenure. No home, family, health or contact details.
