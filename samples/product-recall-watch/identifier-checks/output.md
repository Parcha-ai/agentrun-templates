- **Brightwell Home Electronics BW-200: recall** (CPSC 27015, published 2026-11-13). Parent-unit batteries can overheat and cause burns. Affected: BW-200 parent units manufactured January 2025 through June 2025; cameras excluded. Check the MM/YYYY label inside the battery door. Stop using affected parent units and request a free replacement battery pack: 888-555-0190, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET Monday through Friday, or www.brightwell.example/recall. Your serial alone cannot establish coverage. [Notice](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/brightwell-home-electronics-recalls-bw-200-video-baby-monitors-due-to-burn-hazard)
