**Regulatory tracker: consumer data privacy** 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-16
California's final regulations take effect January 1, 2027; the FTC health-app rule remains proposed.

- **California Privacy Protection Agency: Final regulations: Consumer Health Signals** (final, California). OAL approved and filed the regulations November 10, 2026. Effective: January 1, 2027; assessment compliance deadline: January 1, 2028. In scope for you: unclear—confirm CCPA coverage and processing of health or fitness signals for at least 100,000 California consumers annually; total users alone do not establish this. _Check coverage and calendar both dates separately._ [document](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/consumer_health_signals.html)

- **Federal Trade Commission: Health App Data Sharing Rule** (proposed, United States federal). Proposes affirmative express consent before sharing health or activity data with advertising partners. Published: November 12, 2026; comments due: January 12, 2027. No effective date is stated. In scope for you: yes, under the proposed coverage for US fitness apps. _Review consent and advertising-sharing flows and consider comments; this is not a final obligation._ [document](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/12/2026-24811/health-app-data-sharing)

- **Federal Trade Commission: Remarks of Commissioner Alvarez at the Privacy Futures Summit** (signal, United States federal). Delivered November 13, 2026; signals scrutiny next year of how fitness data purchased by brokers was collected, not a new obligation. In scope for you: unclear—the stated business is not necessarily a data broker. _Check partner roles and supporting collection and consent records._ [document](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/speeches/remarks-commissioner-data-brokers-2026)
