## Recommendation and next steps
First confirm the legal route and company permissions. Then obtain a costed partner proposal, interview corridor users, benchmark matched quotes and build contribution economics. Release launch funding only after compliance and operational testing pass. Move toward go if these gates clear; reject or postpone the six-month objective if no lawful, commercially viable route meets it.
