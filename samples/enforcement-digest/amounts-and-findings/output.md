**Enforcement digest** 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-16 · US broker-dealers · AML
Crestmoor received a $4,000,000 civil money penalty, including $1,000,000 suspended subject to undertakings. Two qualifying actions in the supplied test data.

- **FinCEN · Crestmoor Clearing Corp.** (civil penalty, 2026-11-13): $4,000,000 civil money penalty; $1,000,000 of that penalty suspended pending completion of undertakings. Crestmoor admitted the failures. What failed: AML monitoring of introducing-firm customers' low-priced securities deposits and liquidations, and SAR filing (June 2020 through December 2023). FinCEN investigated following a FINRA referral. Required: independent consultant and SAR lookback covering 2022 and 2023. _Check:_ Test monitoring coverage for introducing-firm customers and whether missed activity warrants a SAR lookback. [release](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-assesses-penalty-against-crestmoor-clearing)

- **SEC · Harborline Securities LLC** (cease-and-desist, 2026-11-12): civil penalty of $1,500,000, disgorgement of $340,000 and prejudgment interest of $41,500. Settled without admitting or denying the SEC's findings. What failed: at least 412 suspicious low-priced securities transactions went unreported while alerts were closed without documented review (January 2021–March 2024). An SEC examination identified the deficiencies. Required: cease-and-desist order, censure and independent AML compliance consultant within 60 days. _Check:_ Sample closed alerts for documented investigation, review and supported SAR decisions. [release](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-188)
