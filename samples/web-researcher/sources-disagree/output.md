## Answer

Three sources give three different heights for the Eldermark Tower in Varnholt:

| Source | Height | What it measures | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| [S1] Tower facts page | **312 m** | Tip of spire (architectural top) | 2026-11-15 (captured) |
| [S2] OpenAtlas wiki | **298 m** | Roof height (excludes spire/antenna) | Last edited 2025-03-04 |
| [S3] Varnholt Courier (2024-05-20) | **330 m** | Total height including new broadcast antenna | Published 2024-05-20 |

### Why they differ
- **S1** quotes the architectural height to the spire tip (312 m), which is the usual "official" height for tall buildings.
- **S2** gives the roof height only (298 m), excluding the spire.
- **S3** reports that a broadcast antenna was added in May 2024, raising the tip to 330 m; antennas are normally excluded from architectural height but counted for total-structure height.

### What remains unconfirmed
- No independent, authoritative source (e.g., CTBUH, Emporis, SkyscraperPage) was found for this tower; all three sources appear to be fictional example sites.
- It is unknown whether the 330 m antenna is still in place as of today (2026-11-15).

**Bottom line:** Depending on the definition, the Eldermark Tower is **298 m (roof)**, **312 m (spire tip)**, or **330 m (antenna tip)** tall.
