# EU residential heat pumps: installer roll-up primer

## Bottom line
- The acquisition thesis is a local installation-and-service business, not simply exposure to equipment growth. Customer complexity, installer shortages and installation costs are documented barriers. **Inference:** execution quality and workforce retention should lead the thesis. [S3]
- Equipment and installed-project market estimates are different revenue measures. Do not add or average them. [S1][S2]
- Fragmentation is reported, but its scale and achievable consolidation benefits remain unverified. [S2]

## Business models
Project revenue combines equipment and installation. Service adds a continuing customer relationship; integrated providers also offer financing, guarantees and energy controls. **Diligence inference:** reconcile equipment pass-through, direct labour, acquisition costs and callbacks before attributing value to scale. Model inventory, project cash timing and guarantee liabilities separately. Manufacturing adds capital requirements beyond installation. [S2][S3][S4][S6][S7]

## Regulation
F-gas guidance addresses qualifications, emissions, applicable leak checks, records and recovery. The revised EPBD entered into force on 2024-05-28; national transposition was due 2026-05-29. It establishes national residential trajectories, not a universal household heat-pump mandate. Country implementation requires separate checking. [S4][S8]

## Trends
Recent evidence shows 2025 demand recovery, January 2025 workforce-policy coordination, Aira's August 2025 financing, January 2026 integrated-energy positioning and the May 2026 EPBD transposition deadline. **Inference:** prioritise country selection, technician retention, affordability and operational integration over a generic EU growth thesis. [S3][S6][S7][S8]

## Glossary
**Air-to-water:** heats a water circuit; **air-to-air:** heats indoor air; **ground-source:** draws ground heat; **installed spending:** equipment plus installation; **F-gases:** regulated fluorinated gases; **EPBD:** building-performance framework; **run-rate:** annualised trading measure; **virtual power plant:** coordinated connected energy devices.
