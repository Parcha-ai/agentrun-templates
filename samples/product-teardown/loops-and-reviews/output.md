## What it does unusually well
Speed receives three separate positive mentions between 2026-08-30 and 2026-10-02. This is repeated praise within four supplied reviews, not a benchmark. Account-free commenting and task-specific template entry points also reduce identifiable participation and discovery friction. [S1][S3][S4]

## Complaints
One review, dated 2026-08-11, reports clunky mobile use and tablet crashes. Treat this as an investigation lead, not a recurring complaint pattern. [S4]

## Takeaways
Separate creator signup from collaborator participation. Tie referral rewards to meaningful outcomes. Connect templates to specific jobs, then measure the signup-to-useful-board handoff rather than celebrating registrations alone. These are analytical lessons from the documented mechanisms, not proven Slatebird results. [S1][S2][S3]
