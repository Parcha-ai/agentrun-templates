**Single-cell RNA sequencing methods weekly** 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-16
The November 10 integration benchmark reports scVI ranking first on 5 of 8 datasets, with no method first across all datasets.

1. **A benchmark of 14 single-cell data integration methods** (Genome Biology Methods · Nov 10)
   Reyes P, Chen L, et al. compare 14 methods on 8 datasets comprising 1.2 million cells using 10 metrics. The authors report that scVI ranks first on 5 of 8 datasets, but no method leads everywhere.
   Only an abstract-level excerpt was available; metric definitions and ranking procedures remain unassessed.
   For researchers: include scVI as a baseline and test method choice against your own datasets. [Source](https://journal.example/genomebio/article/10.5555/gb.2026.118)

2. **ScaleCell: a foundation model for cell-type annotation across tissues** (bioRxiv · Nov 11), Preprint (not peer-reviewed)
   Okafor A, Lindqvist M, Tanaka R, et al. introduce a transformer pretrained on 48 million cells from 31 tissues. The authors report a mean adjusted Rand index of 0.71 on 12 held-out datasets, against 0.64 for scANVI, their strongest existing comparator.
   Abstract only; the PDF is unavailable. The associated press claim that this 'solves cell annotation' is not established by these benchmark results.
   For researchers: consider it a candidate baseline, but await full methods before judging transferability and evaluation leakage. [Source](https://biorxiv.example/content/10.1101/2026.11.09.551234v1)

3. **Human Lung Development Atlas v1** (Zenodo · Nov 13)
   Hartmann Lab releases raw counts and annotations under CC-BY 4.0. The authors report 2.1 million cells from 64 donors.
   Record only; assay details, developmental-stage coverage and annotation validation are not provided in the supplied excerpt.
   For researchers: inspect donor and assay metadata before building transfer or integration benchmarks. [Source](https://zenodo.example/records/1188822)

4. **RFA-HG-27-004: Scalable tools for single-cell data analysis (U01)** (Grants Guide · Nov 9)
