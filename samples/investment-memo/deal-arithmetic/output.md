# Saltmarsh Robotics: IC memo draft, 2026-11-14

*Interpretation:* Proposed Series A; assume dollars are USD and full primary subscription. [M] denotes supplied, unverified terms. Apply a generic venture bar; fund fit remains for the partner to confirm. This is a diligence draft, not investment advice.

## Bottom line
**Hold for diligence, not approval.** Saltmarsh says it leases autonomous weeders to organic vegetable farms [S1]. The proposed investment buys 4.0% before additional dilution [M; calculation below]. The central question is whether seasonal receipts cover fleet capital and field support while delivering grower savings. Commercial scale and economics are not established.

## Summary and thesis
- Confirm that seasonal leasing reduces adoption friction without undermining asset returns [S1].
- Confirm differentiation against existing robotic weed-control alternatives [S4, S5].
- Confirm that a regional organic-farming wedge can expand into a venture-scale market [S2, S7].

## Team
The website identifies co-founders Elena Brooks, CEO and formerly a robotics engineer at a farm equipment maker, and Tom Hale, CTO [S1]. These are company claims; employer details, delivery histories and operational leadership depth were not independently established.

## Market — method
Defra reports **8.4 thousand hectares** of organic vegetables in England in 2025, including potatoes and land in conversion [S7]. This is not confirmed compatible acreage. The UK's **5,004 organic operators** include non-target businesses and should not be treated as customers [S3].

Revenue-sizing formula: compatible hectares ÷ hectares served per robot-season × adoption × paid months × advertised $2,500 monthly lease [S1]. Capacity, compatibility, adoption and season length are missing; **revenue TAM is not found**.

## Product
The company advertises autonomous weeding robots at **$2,500 per robot per month during the growing season** [S1]. Currency, service inclusions, mechanism, throughput, supervision and safety evidence are unspecified. Seasonal access is a commercial feature, not demonstrated technical defensibility.

## Deal and terms
Proposed raise **$10M at $40M pre-money**, including our **$2M** [M]. Post-money = $40M + $10M = **$50M**; ownership = $2M ÷ $50M = **4.0%**. This excludes incremental pool, convertible or other dilution. Cap table, preferences and definitive documents are missing. Neither a revenue multiple nor a reliable comp-price judgment is possible.
